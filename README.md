Heatwave Cover Studio — turn your own photos into GTA 6-style poster cover art. Seven multi-panel layouts (new Key Art hero poster and 3×3 Mosaic, plus Cover Grid, Five Panel, Triptych, Single Hero, Split Diptych), eight Vice City colour grades (new Heatwave and Ultraviolet gradient-map grades, plus Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Palm Dusk, Golden Hour, Monochrome Noir, Miami Pop), five distinct bundled sample scenes with a different photo in every panel, per-panel crop and positioning by dragging on the live preview, bold Bebas Neue caption lettering with a new halftone style and a title band, and one-click PNG export in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. Fully offline: static files only, no network, your photos never leave your machine. Everything is original — no Rockstar logo, artwork or fonts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Add photos (file picker or drag-and-drop), pick a layout and grade, drag a panel to reposition its photo, scroll to zoom, edit the caption, and export a PNG in any of the three post sizes — or all three at once.
