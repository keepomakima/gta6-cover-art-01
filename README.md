Key Art Studio — turn any photo and a caption into GTA 6-style cover art: multi-panel posters, sunset gradient-map grades, retro-chrome 3D titles, poster-authentic details (billing block, seal, callouts, role tags, gold frame, sky glow), PNG export in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6 Cover Art" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the app runs fully offline in the browser; photos never leave your machine.

## How it works

1. Pick a layout (8: Key Art, Mosaic 9-panel, Cover Grid, Five Panel, Triptych, Single Hero, Split Diptych, Slanted) and a sunset grade (8 gradient-map grades, Heatwave by default).
2. Add your photos (upload or drag-drop) or use the 5 built-in sample scenes; click a panel, then drag, scroll or slide to crop and position each photo.
3. Write the title (retro-chrome 3D, halftone or flat ink) and subtitle, toggle the poster details (seal, billing block, callouts, role tags, gold frame, sky glow).
4. Export PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080 — or all three at once.

Everything is original: the sample scenes are drawn procedurally in-session, the lettering is Bebas Neue under the SIL Open Font License, and there is no Rockstar logo, artwork or font.
